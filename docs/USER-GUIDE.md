# CareRoute 0.2 — simple user guide

CareRoute keeps the work around a planned hospital visit in one place. Families prepare their questions. Doctors document their own findings and instructions. Coordinators track reports, appointments and changes. The current app demonstrates those workflows using fictional records on your device.

## Start

Open the website and choose Explore the demo. No app account is created. The hosting provider may ask the owner to sign in to access this private website. Choose Light, Dark or System from the theme menu. The choice is remembered on the device.

## Patients and families

1. Choose Patient view using the Demo role selector. Choose one of the fictional patients.
2. Open Prepare for visit. Write fictional concerns, current medicines, allergies and questions. Tick the preparation checklist.
3. Open a care journey to see its steps, missing information and appointment details.
4. Save a visit brief, download a calendar or print/share the itinerary. These actions do not book a hospital appointment or contact anyone.

A completed administrative checklist is not permission to travel or medical clearance. Confirm the visit with the real hospital independently.

## Doctors

Use the coordinator demonstration and open Doctor workbench. This is not a verified doctor account.

- **Overview:** search the fictional scheduled consultations and open a patient's notes or care plan.
- **Consultation:** enter history, examination, allergies, current medicines, assessment, plan and referral notes. Review the patient's prepared brief. Changes save on this device as you type; export a text copy if needed.
- **Prescription:** choose the patient, enter clinician and clinic details, then type every medicine instruction yourself. Required fields include name, strength, dose, route, frequency, duration and quantity. Add advice and a follow-up date. Acknowledge your review and preview the draft. Print it on the web or share a text draft on Android. Every output says DRAFT — NOT VALID FOR DISPENSING. The app does not verify credentials, recommend medicines, check interactions or confirm safe doses. Changed drafts require review again.
- **Follow-ups:** add a dated task, mark it complete or delete it. This is a local checklist; no reminder is sent and no patient is monitored.

This covers selected documentation and coordination work. It does not cover every activity of every specialist. Clinical examination, diagnosis, treatment decisions and issuing a valid prescription remain outside this demonstration.

## Coordinators

Open a care journey, record prerequisites and appointment steps, assign a responsible fictional staff member and review missing information. Record delays; review affected timing and confirm an updated plan after checking it. No real hospital schedule is changed. Activity history records demonstration actions.

## Public resources

- **Pune directory:** search 16 facility listings and 344 doctor listings from Ruby Hall Clinic's public directory. Filter facilities by area, save listings and open official source or map-search links. This is not every doctor or clinic in Pune. Confirm contact details, qualifications, fees, location and availability with the provider.
- **First-aid guide:** read four short educational cards and their original sources. The visible emergency link calls 112 in India. No symptom analysis or personalised treatment is provided. The cards are not a full first-aid course.
- **Source library:** open professional guidance and original information. No AI model was trained and no claim of complete medical knowledge is made.

## Saving and deleting

Records save in browser/app local storage, not a secure medical-record database. Use fictional data only. Another tab on the same website can update the displayed data. If a stale-save message appears, review the latest values and repeat your change. Local checks are not a substitute for a server database with transactions.

Settings can export records, reset sample data or delete the demo records and added notes/drafts/tasks/bookmarks. Patient exports omit clinician notes, prescription drafts and staff tasks. Role switching is still only a demonstration; it is not a security boundary. Downloaded, printed and shared files must be deleted separately. Theme preference can remain after deleting records.

## What this release does not provide

No real patient accounts, secure shared records, online booking, teleconsultation, payment, live hospital integrations, automatic notifications, drug database, clinical AI or validated medical decision support. No physical Android-device test has yet been completed. Complete the production plan before a real clinical pilot or sale as an operational medical service.
